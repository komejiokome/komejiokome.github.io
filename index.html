INFINITAS Playlog Viewer

INFINITAS Playlog Viewer is a personal, local companion tool for reviewing beatmania IIDX INFINITAS play history and practice results.

Its optional video-publishing feature watches a user-selected local recording folder and, after a recording is confirmed complete, uploads that recording to the user's own YouTube account as a private video. The feature uses Google's OAuth 2.0 flow and requests only the YouTube upload permission required for this purpose.

The application runs locally on the user's computer. It does not operate a developer-hosted backend for collecting play history, recordings, or OAuth tokens.
